Abstract

Human-robot motion retargeting is a technology that maps human movements to robot movements. It has emerged as a pivotal function in embodied intelligence, particularly in domains involving interaction and grasping, such as the teleoperation of dexterous robotic hands. Most of the existing models mainly focus on single-frame retargeting, often ignoring the inter-frame dependencies, which results in the generated robot retargeting outcomes lacking smoothness. In this paper, we propose TransRetarget, a novel multi-frame human-robot hand motion retargeting method based on a temporal-spatial Transformer architecture. By leveraging the powerful modeling capabilities of Transformers, our method can effectively capture the complex relationships between human hand poses and robotic hand actions. It can not only comprehensively model the intra-frame joint correlations, but also capture inter-frame temporal dependencies, thereby generating high- quality robotic hand motion data. Furthermore, to ensure action consistency between human hand poses and robotic hand actions, we design a suite of loss functions, including finger side movement, finger flexion, and fingertip distance, which are integrated into a composite loss function for training. Experimental results demonstrate that TransRetarget achieves superior performance across multiple datasets and various dexterous robotic hand platforms.
